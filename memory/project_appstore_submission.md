App Store submission is in progress but blocked. Build 49 (v1.0.0) is uploaded and attached to the 1.0 draft. The "Clique Verified" subscription group exists with "Clique Verified Monthly" (Clique_Verified_Monthly, 1 month) ready for review.

**Why:** First-time App Store submission for Clique – Watch with Friends under Vaulted Media Group LLC.

**How to apply:** When resuming, go to App Store Connect → Clique → Distribution → 1.0 Prepare for Submission and work through the checklist below.

## Outstanding blockers (all required before Submit for Review):

1. **Screenshots** — need at least one for 6.5-inch iPhone (1242×2688px or 1284×2778px)
2. **Privacy Policy URL** — go to App Privacy section
3. **Age ratings** — go to App Information → answer content questions
4. **Primary category** — App Information → pick category (e.g. Social Networking)
5. **Contact Information** — name/email for Apple review team
6. **Copyright** — e.g. "2026 Vaulted Media Group LLC"
7. **Description** — App Store description (English U.S.)
8. **Keywords** — comma-separated search terms
9. **Support URL** — URL where users can get help
